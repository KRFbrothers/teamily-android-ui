Teamily Design System

Brand tokens, type scale and accessibility notes for the Teamily AI Android app. Every value below is taken from the shipped design.

Typography

  • Lato 400 — body and snippets
  • Lato 700 — titles, buttons, row names
  • Lato 900 — display, section labels, numerals

Accessibility

  • Every touch target is at least 48 px.
  • Mint #42d982 is fill only — never text. On the light canvas it measures 1.62:1.
  • Body text on canvas measures 14.91:1 in day and 15.25:1 in night.
